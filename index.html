Questions

Frequently Asked

Is this coaching in-person or online? +
Both options are available. Local clients in the San Tan Valley / East Valley area can meet in person. Clients outside the area work with Michael via Zoom with the same level of intensity and accountability.
I've tried therapy and self-help books. How is this different? +
Therapy looks backward. This coaching looks forward. We don't process trauma for months — we build new behavior, new habits, and new identity through action. The martial arts approach means you're tested, challenged, and held accountable in real time.
I'm very introverted and shy. Is this program right for me? +
Yes — this was built for you. Being introverted is not a flaw, but letting fear run your life is a choice you can change. We work at your pace with structured, measurable steps so you never feel thrown in the deep end.
What if I'm not ready to commit to a full program yet? +
The free strategy call is for exactly this reason. No commitment, no sales pressure — just 30 minutes to see if it's the right fit. "Not ready" is often the feeling that means you're exactly ready.
Do I need any martial arts experience? +
Absolutely not. The martial arts background informs Michael's coaching philosophy — discipline, resilience, presence — but you'll never be asked to spar or step on a mat (unless you want to).
How many clients does Michael take at one time? +
To maintain quality, Michael works with no more than 8 active coaching clients at a time. Spots are limited. When the calendar shows availability, it's real — act fast.
